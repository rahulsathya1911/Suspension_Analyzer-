3. **Improved Toast Notifications**: Slide from right with backdrop blur, better shadows and rounded corners
4. **Loading Spinners**: Animated rotating borders on loading buttons
5. **Flash Animations**: Enhanced glow effects with box-shadows on metric updates
6. **Button Hover States**: Shimmer effects, better disabled/enabled visual contrast
